# The official Magic card back

The generator never prints the official Magic: The Gathering card back, nor a back drawn to look like it (its oval, its swirl, its logo or its lettering).

## Why this is out of scope

A proxy with the official back is, in the hand, a counterfeit card: nothing tells it apart from a real one once sleeved or shuffled. Wizards' Fan Content Policy already leaves proxies out of fan content (`research/proxy.md`); a back that copies Wizards' trade dress moves the project from "private playtest tool" to "counterfeit maker", which is the one risk that would end it.

Duplex sheets still need a uniform back, so cards cannot be told apart when shuffled. That need is met by the project's own neutral back (see `GLOSSARY.md`, _retro neutro_): it may echo the colours of a Magic back, but its shapes are its own and it says "PROXY".
