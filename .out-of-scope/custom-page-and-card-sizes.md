# Landscape pages, custom page sizes and custom card sizes

The sheet prints portrait, on A4 or Letter, with cards the size of a real Magic card. There is no orientation switch, no custom page size and no custom card size.

## Why this is out of scope

- **Landscape** holds fewer cards: on A4 a portrait page holds 3×3 cards, a landscape one 4×2.
- **Custom page sizes** serve print shops and larger printers; the users the generator is for (the player and the enthusiast, see `GLOSSARY.md`) print at home, on A4 or Letter.
- **Custom card sizes** serve other card games. The generator makes Magic proxies, and a Magic card has one size.

Proxxied and other tools offer all three; comparing them with this project's users, they add settings nobody here needs.
