# 0004: Scryfall images are never altered

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-04), amended (2026-11-07): images cover the slot instead of fitting inside it, clipped to the card's rounded outline

## Decision

Card images are placed in the PDF exactly as Scryfall serves them, scaled uniformly to cover the 63×88 mm slot, centred, and clipped to the slot. No stretching, no colour changes, no watermarks, no bleed generated by extending or mirroring the image. The copyright line and artist credit are never cropped: clipping only ever removes the sliver of outer border that overflows the slot.

The clipping path is the card's real outline, the shape it is cut and sleeved to: 63×88 mm with 3 mm rounded corners (measured on Scryfall's images: about 2.9 mm). Scryfall's JPGs already draw that curve, but paint the corners outside it white or black depending on the print. Clipping lets the paper show there, so every card on the sheet has the same shape and no black fills the corners between cards. Scryfall's own PNG format leaves those corners transparent, so this adds nothing Scryfall does not already treat as outside the card.

Scryfall's "large" JPGs are 672×936 px, a touch wider than a card (0.7179 against 0.7159). Covering the slot gives 63.18×88 mm and clips about 0.09 mm of outer border on each side.

## Why

- Scryfall's image guidelines forbid cropping the copyright or artist, distorting, recolouring, or watermarking images (see `research/proxy.md`).
- The project is a free, unofficial fan tool for private playtesting; staying inside Scryfall's and Wizards' policies is what keeps it viable (see `research/competitor.md`).

## Considered options

- **Fit inside the slot** (the original decision): the image comes out 63×87.75 mm with 0.125 mm of blank paper above and below. On white-bordered cards the blank merges with the card edge, so the printed card is short and its edge is hard to find when cutting.
- **Stretch to 63×88 mm**: a 0.28% non-uniform scale, invisible in print, but Scryfall's image rules forbid distorting or stretching their images, and repeated misuse can get the API blocked.
- **Cover and clip** (chosen): exactly 63×88 mm, uniform scale, and the only pixels lost are outer border, which Scryfall's rules do not protect.

## Consequences

- Bleed stays out of scope until its compatibility with Scryfall's policy is confirmed.
- Layout customisation applies only to the page (margins, spacing, crop marks), never to the image.
- Custom uploaded images, if ever added, are the user's responsibility and follow a separate decision.
