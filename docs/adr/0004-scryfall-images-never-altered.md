# 0004: Scryfall images are never altered

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-04)

## Decision

Card images are placed in the PDF exactly as Scryfall serves them, scaled uniformly to 63×88 mm. No cropping (copyright line and artist credit stay visible), no stretching, no colour changes, no watermarks, no bleed generated by extending or mirroring the image.

## Why

- Scryfall's image guidelines forbid cropping the copyright or artist, distorting, recolouring, or watermarking images (see `research/proxy.md`).
- The project is a free, unofficial fan tool for private playtesting; staying inside Scryfall's and Wizards' policies is what keeps it viable (see `research/competitor.md`).

## Consequences

- Bleed stays out of scope until its compatibility with Scryfall's policy is confirmed.
- Layout customisation applies only to the page (margins, spacing, crop marks), never to the image.
- Custom uploaded images, if ever added, are the user's responsibility and follow a separate decision.
