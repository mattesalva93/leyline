# 0007: Images cover the slot, clipped to the card's outline

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-07, recorded 2026-11-09); supersedes ADR 0004

## Decision

Card images are placed in the PDF as Scryfall serves them, scaled uniformly to cover the slot, centred, and clipped to the outline a real card is cut and sleeved along: the slot with rounded corners. Clipping only ever removes outer border, never the copyright line or the artist credit. No stretching, no colour changes, no watermarks, no bleed generated by extending or mirroring the image.

The geometry is pinned by the tests in `packages/core/src/sheet-pdf.test.ts`: "fills the slot with a Scryfall "large" image, scaled uniformly, trimming only the side edges" and "cuts every card to the shape of a real card".

## Why

- Scryfall's image guidelines forbid cropping the copyright or artist, distorting, recolouring, or watermarking images (see `research/proxy.md`). Outer border is not protected.
- Scryfall's images are slightly wider than a card, so fitting them inside the slot left a strip of blank paper above and below each card. On white-bordered cards the strip merges with the border, and the card comes out short and hard to cut.
- Scryfall's JPGs paint the corners outside the card's curve white or black depending on the print. Clipping lets the paper show there, so every card has the same shape. Scryfall's PNGs leave those corners transparent, so this removes nothing Scryfall treats as part of the card.
- The project is a free, unofficial fan tool for private playtesting; staying inside Scryfall's and Wizards' policies is what keeps it viable (see `research/competitor.md`).

## Considered options

- **Fit inside the slot** (ADR 0004): no pixel lost, but a short card with blank strips.
- **Stretch to the slot**: invisible in print, but Scryfall's rules forbid distorting images, and repeated misuse can get the API blocked.
- **Cover and clip** (chosen): exact card size, uniform scale, and the only pixels lost are outer border.

## Consequences

- Bleed stays out of scope until its compatibility with Scryfall's policy is confirmed.
- Layout customisation applies only to the page (margins, spacing, crop marks), never to the image.
- Custom uploaded images, if ever added, are the user's responsibility and follow a separate decision.
